Draggable Widget

You can drag the widget to any position on your desktop. Just hold the Alt key while clicking and dragging it.

Click-Through Mode

When "Click Through" is enabled, mouse clicks will pass directly through the widget onto the desktop or folder underneath it. This prevents the widget from blocking desktop items.

Note: To disable click-through or re-enter settings once click-through is enabled, double-click or right-click the green battery icon in your Windows System Tray (bottom right).

Real-Time System Monitoring

You can assign any of the 4 circles to monitor your PC resources in real-time:

  • CPU: Displays system-wide processor workload.
  • RAM: Displays percentage of used system memory.
  • Laptop Battery: Real-time device charge level and charging indicator.
  • Bluetooth: Shows battery levels of connected devices (headphones, mouse) that report stats to Windows.
